Hugging Face models: which inference engine can run them?

Top 500 in each of three rankings, trending, most liked, and most downloaded, classified by task (pipeline_tag) and mapped to a serving engine. HF API, May 2026. Companion to the OpenRouter report, same infaas lens.

Bottom line: across all three rankings, vLLM serves only ~55-59% of the catalog (text + vision-language + embeddings). Hugging Face is deeply multimodal: image/video generation, speech, and classic CV make up the rest. The three rankings stress different engines, trending and liked are diffusion-heavy (image models are popular), while downloaded is embedding/encoder-heavy (tiny BERT/MiniLM workhorses pulled into pipelines). A real platform here is multi-engine: vLLM + Triton + diffusers + a speech path.

5. Recommendation for infaas

  • vLLM is the biggest single lane (~55-59%) but never a majority of the whole catalog. It is necessary, not sufficient.
  • Triton is essential as the multi-backend home for classic CV, encoders/BERT, and as a Python-backend wrapper for the rest.
  • Add diffusers/ComfyUI if you serve the trending/liked frontier (image & video generation is ~20% there).
  • Add a speech path (faster-whisper / NeMo) for the steady ~9-10% ASR/TTS share across all rankings.
  • Downloads skew to embeddings/encoders, so a lightweight TEI or vLLM-pooling endpoint covers a lot of real production traffic cheaply.
  • Every HF model is open-weight, so coverage is purely an engine-capability question, no "API only" gap.
